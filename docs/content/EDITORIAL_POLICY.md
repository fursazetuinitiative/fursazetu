# Purpose

This document defines the editorial principles, ethical standards, and governance framework that guide all content published on FursaZetu.

Its purpose is to ensure every opportunity published on the platform is accurate, trustworthy, relevant, and aligned with FursaZetu's mission of connecting people to genuine opportunities for growth.

This policy applies to all contributors, curators, editors, administrators, and any technology used to assist in the publication process.



# Our Mission

FursaZetu exists to reduce the information gap between opportunity providers and opportunity seekers.

We curate opportunities that enable individuals and communities to learn, work, lead, innovate, compete, and grow.

Every published opportunity should help users make informed decisions with confidence.



# Editorial Philosophy

FursaZetu is a curated opportunity platform.

We do not publish content simply because it exists.

We publish opportunities because they are relevant, credible, and valuable to our audience.

Quality will always take precedence over quantity.

Trust will always take precedence over speed.



# Core Editorial Principles

## 1. Accuracy

Information must reflect the official source as accurately as possible.

Facts should never be altered, exaggerated, or presented out of context.



## 2. Verification

Every opportunity should be verified using the official source whenever reasonably possible.

Official sources include:

- Official websites
- Government portals
- University websites
- Organization websites
- Official application portals

Unofficial reposts should never be treated as primary sources.



## 3. Transparency

Users deserve to know where information originates.

Every opportunity should clearly identify:

- Source organization
- Official application link
- Publication date (when available)
- Deadline
- Verification status



## 4. Neutrality

FursaZetu informs.

It does not persuade.

Content should remain objective and factual without promotional language or unnecessary hype.



## 5. Accessibility

Information should be understandable to readers from different educational and professional backgrounds.

Complex announcements should be simplified without changing their meaning.



## 6. Timeliness

Publishing opportunities before deadlines is essential.

Expired opportunities should be archived promptly to maintain the quality and credibility of the platform.



## 7. User Value

Every published opportunity should answer one question:

"Does this genuinely help our audience grow?"

If the answer is no, it should not be published.



# Opportunity Acceptance Policy

An opportunity may be published only if it meets the following criteria:

✓ Relevant to the FursaZetu audience

✓ Published by a credible organization

✓ Contains sufficient information

✓ Includes an official application method

✓ Has a valid deadline or application period

✓ Complies with applicable laws and ethical standards



# Opportunities We Will Not Publish

FursaZetu will not knowingly publish:

- Fraudulent opportunities
- Pyramid schemes
- Misleading advertisements
- Gambling promotions
- Adult content
- Hate speech
- Discriminatory content
- Opportunities lacking a credible source
- Content that infringes intellectual property rights
- Opportunities intended to deceive users

Protecting our audience is a core responsibility.



# The Role of a Curator

Curators are responsible for maintaining the quality and integrity of the platform.

Their responsibilities include:

- Discovering opportunities
- Verifying information
- Summarizing official announcements
- Maintaining consistency
- Updating information when necessary
- Removing expired opportunities
- Reporting questionable content

Curators should always prioritize the interests of users.



# AI-Assisted Curation

Artificial intelligence may assist with:

- Summarization
- Formatting
- Grammar
- SEO recommendations
- Metadata generation
- Draft preparation

AI must not replace human judgment.

Every AI-assisted publication should be reviewed before publication.

Responsibility for published content remains with the curator.



# Corrections Policy

Mistakes should be corrected promptly.

When significant factual errors are identified:

- Verify the correction.
- Update the content.
- Record the change internally.

Maintaining trust is more important than avoiding corrections.



# Content Removal Policy

Content may be removed when:

- The opportunity is fraudulent.
- Information is proven false.
- The application period has permanently ended.
- The official source requests removal where appropriate.
- Publication violates legal or ethical obligations.

Whenever possible, archived content should remain distinguishable from active opportunities.



# Conflict of Interest

Curators should remain impartial.

Personal relationships, financial interests, or external influence must never affect editorial decisions.

Editorial independence is essential to maintaining public trust.

# Editorial Independence

FursaZetu retains full editorial discretion over what is published.

Acceptance of partnerships, sponsorships, or collaborations must never compromise editorial integrity.

Users should always be able to distinguish between editorial content and promotional material.



# Guiding Principle

Every opportunity published on FursaZetu should save users time, reduce uncertainty, and increase confidence.

If an opportunity does not help someone move closer to growth, it does not belong on FursaZetu.

FursaZetu exists to be **Your Curated Link to Growth.**
